EXPERT GUIDANCE

FITNESS QUESTIONS? WE CAN HELP.

Find answers to common questions about training frequency, proper home setup, injury prevention, and building sustainable workout habits.

For most healthy adults, major health organizations recommend accumulating at least 150 minutes of moderate aerobic activity or 75 minutes of vigorous exercise each week, alongside muscle-strengthening activities on 2 or more days. A balanced ELEVE8 routine typically consists of 3 to 4 focused training days coupled with daily gentle walks and morning mobility flows.

Absolutely. Calisthenics and bodyweight exercises can create immense progressive tension through leverage shifts, mechanical tempo changes, and increased range of motion. Combined with the ELEVE8 workout timer and voice coach, you can achieve remarkable cardiovascular conditioning, muscular tone, and functional flexibility in your living room.

Beginners should begin with our "Foundational Strength 101" and "Morning Full Body Warm-Up" routines. Start with 10 to 15 minutes per session, 3 times a week. The most important initial metric is showing up consistently rather than pushing yourself to exhaustion.

Always complete 5 to 8 minutes of dynamic warm-ups (such as joint rotations, arm sweeps, and light bodyweight squats) to elevate core body temperature and prepare joint capsules. Drink a glass of water, ensure proper footwear and clear workout space, and listen to the voice coach's introductory cues.

Habit stacking is key: anchor your exercise to an existing routine, such as doing your 10-minute morning workout immediately after making your bed. Track your completed workouts, prioritize fun movements, and remember that a 10-minute workout done today is infinitely better than an imaginary 1-hour session tomorrow.

Morning routines are designed to stimulate adrenaline, elevate heart rate, and clear mental sluggishness for the upcoming workday. Evening routines focus on parasympathetic activation, spinal decompression, and cortisol reduction to support restful sleep. You can freely combine a 10-minute morning session with an 8-minute evening stretch!

Rest is when muscles rebuild and neuromuscular adaptation takes place. Schedule at least 1 to 2 dedicated recovery days weekly. If you experience persistent lethargy, sharp joint pain, or elevated resting heart rate, take an extra rest day or switch to gentle mobility breathing.

Medical & Safety Advisory:

Always consult your physician before beginning any new exercise or physical conditioning regimen, especially if you have pre-existing cardiovascular, musculoskeletal, or metabolic conditions. Immediately stop exercising if you experience chest tightness, dizziness, sharp pain, or shortness of breath, and seek prompt professional medical assistance.
